Asset Management System

My local folder structure traces straight back to the rows in the Asset Plan tables, so anyone opening it can find an asset without me walking them through it.

KeepersWatch_Folio/
├── 01_Reference/          (moodboard exports, research PDFs, citations)
├── 02_3D/
│   ├── LighthouseLamp/    (source .blend, exported .fbx, textures/)
│   ├── Rowboat/
│   ├── LobsterTrap/
│   ├── PierPost/
│   └── SeagullNest/
├── 03_Audio/
│   ├── Raw/               (unedited source recordings/downloads)
│   └── Final/             (edited, folio-ready .wav files)
├── 04_Documentation/       (asset list .xlsx, timeline .xlsx, working doc)
└── 05_Exports/             (final render screenshots, Sketchfab export bundles)

Naming convention: AssetType_AssetName_Version — e.g. 3D_LighthouseLamp_v2.blend, AUD_Foghorn_Ambient_Final.wav. No generic names ("new folder," "stuff," "test"). Every folder/file name traces directly back to a row in the Asset Plan tables.

Risk & Plan B

I haven't used Blender before this unit, so the biggest risk is the Lighthouse Lamp Housing (the hero, most complex asset) taking longer than the Week 5–6 window allows while I'm still learning box modelling basics. Plan B is already built into the asset list — the Lobster Trap and Pier Post are scoped to lean on texture detail rather than modelled geometry, so if the Lamp Housing overruns, time can be pulled from those simpler props without cutting an asset entirely.
