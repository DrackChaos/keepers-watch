Reference List

Almeida, G.C. de, Andrade, A.O. and Vargas, P.A., 2023. Spatial audio in virtual reality: a systematic review. In: Proceedings of the 22nd Brazilian Symposium on Games and Digital Entertainment. New York: ACM. https://doi.org/10.1145/3625008.3625042

This is what convinced me the sonic moodboard isn't a side task. Spatial audio measurably affects presence, so ambient layering — the direction the foghorn comes from, how the waves sit relative to the wind — is core design work here, not polish added at the end.

Audacity Team, n.d. Editing audio. [online] Available at: support.audacityteam.org/basics/audacity-editing [Accessed 26 Jul. 2026].

Going through the core workflow here — import, trim, layer — before I touch any raw recordings, so I'm not guessing my way through Audacity once Folio 2 production actually starts.

Eggers, R. (dir.), 2019. The Lighthouse [Film]. USA: A24.

Referenced for how it lights weathered material — heavy shadow, one hard light source doing all the work. I want the same in the scene: the lamp itself as the dominant light, not even ambient lighting everywhere.

Lamerichs, N., 2016. The meaning of the island in art game Dear Esther. [online] Available at: nicollelamerichs.com/2016/03/20/making-sense-of-indie-game-dear-esther [Accessed 26 Jul. 2026].

This is what pushed me toward environmental storytelling instead of a guided tour. The island in Dear Esther tells its story through what's been left behind, not dialogue — that's the approach I want for what happened to the keeper.

Lighthouses of Australia, n.d. Point Lonsdale Lighthouse. [online] Available at: lighthouses.org.au/vic/point-lonsdale-lighthouse [Accessed 26 Jul. 2026].

Used for real proportions — how tall the tower actually is relative to the lamp room, how big the lamp housing is next to a person — so the Lighthouse Lamp Housing isn't just guessed at.

Meta, n.d. Testing and performance analysis — Meta Horizon OS Developers. [online] Available at: developers.meta.com/horizon/documentation/unity/unity-perf [Accessed 26 Jul. 2026].

Source of the 400–600 draw call / 1.3–1.8M triangle numbers on the Asset Plan page. I didn't want to guess at a "reasonable" polycount — I wanted the actual figure Meta recommends for this hardware.

Poeschl, S. and Doering, N., 2013. Integration of spatial sound in immersive virtual environments: an experimental study on effects of spatial sound on presence. In: 2013 IEEE Virtual Reality (VR). IEEE, pp.129–130. DOI: 10.1109/VR.2013.6549396. Available at: researchgate.net [IEEE Xplore's own page for this DOI is currently down; ResearchGate mirror used instead].

A second, independent study backing the same conclusion as the Almeida review above. Two separate papers landing on the same point gave me more confidence it's not a one-off finding.

Price, A., n.d. Blender Guru [YouTube channel]. Available at: youtube.com/@blenderguru [Accessed 26 Jul. 2026].

The tutorial series I'm actually going to work through on a practice object before touching a real prop — see the Reflection page for why.

Road to VR, 2024. Quest 3S vs Quest 3 vs Quest 2 compared with detailed specs. Hayden, S. [online] Available at: roadtovr.com/quest-3s-quest-3-quest-2-specs-compared [Accessed 26 Jul. 2026].

Confirmed the Quest 3S shares its chipset and RAM with the Quest 3. That's the entire reason I can use Meta's Quest 3 performance figures for a 3S I actually own, instead of guessing at different numbers.

The Chinese Room, 2012. Dear Esther [Video game]. UK: The Chinese Room.

The main reference for the whole project, listed separately from Lamerichs' analysis above because I spent time playing it myself, not just reading about it — the pacing of discovery is what I'm trying to replicate.
