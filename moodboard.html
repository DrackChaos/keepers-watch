Visual Moodboard

Art style: Stylised low-poly with hand-painted-look textures (flat colour + baked shading, no PBR realism) — a deliberate choice that fits the Quest 3S triangle budget (see Asset Plan) and matches the painterly, slightly unreal tone of Dear Esther's island. Everything below — the palette, the typography, and the reference photography — is one board: five colours pulled from a foggy, lamp-lit coastline, paired with a display serif for the "voice of the keeper" and a clean sans for readability, then grounded against the actual materials (glazing, rust, driftwood) those choices are meant to evoke. The same five colours run through this website itself, so the site is built in the palette, not just describing it.

Palette: Navy · Teal · Fog · Amber · Driftwood
