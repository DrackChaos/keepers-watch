Asset Plan

Photo: paws and prints / Unsplash

I've scoped every prop below against the Quest 3S's actual performance headroom — 400–600 draw calls, 1.3–1.8M triangles scene-wide (Meta, n.d.; the same budget carries over to the 3S per Road to VR, 2024). That's the whole reason these polycounts stay low.

Planned Visual (3D) Assets

Asset nameCategoryDescriptionTarget poly countTexture size
Lighthouse Lamp HousingHero prop The rotating lamp mechanism at the top of the tower — the focal object of the scene. ~4,000 tris2K (diffuse + emissive for the beam)
Weathered RowboatEnvironment prop Beached, half-sunk wooden rowboat on the rocks below the cliff. ~1,800 tris1K
Lobster TrapDetail prop Timber-and-rope trap left on the pier, hand interaction target. Simplified from the original plan — rope weave and wood weathering are baked into the texture rather than modelled as geometry, since this is a background prop, not the hero asset. ~900 tris512×512
Pier PostStructural prop Repeating weathered timber post used to dress the pier walkway. Same simplification — weathering/damage detail comes from texture, not modelled variation per post. ~400 tris512×512
Seagull Nest (stretch, 5th asset)Detail prop Small nest on a cliff ledge, adds life/scale to the environment. ~600 tris512×512

I've planned five props instead of the required four. The Seagull Nest is the one I can cut if time gets tight — more on that call on the Reflection page.
