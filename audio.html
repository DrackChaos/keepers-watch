Audio Assets — Final Folio

Photo: Alex Moliski / Unsplash

8 finished audio assets, each sourced from a raw recording (own field recordings or CC0 Freesound downloads — full licence/source list on the Asset Management page) and edited into a folio-ready clip. Two of the originally planned 11 (both Seagull Calls, Pier Wood Creak) were deliberately dropped: their raw sources were already isolated down to 0.4–2.2 seconds by the original uploader, leaving nothing meaningful left to edit in the software used here. The Scene Integration page shows 4 of the ambient beds below layered together into one mixed-down preview.

Ambient beds

Continuous, loopable background layers — each trimmed to a clean loop point with a short crossfade so it repeats without a click or gap.

Foghorn Ambient
Source: own recording/download. Edited: trimmed to one clean blast + the natural gap after it, short fade-out on the tail, normalised.
Ocean Waves Loop
Source: "sea loop.wav" by PospecStudio — Freesound ↗ (CC0). Edited: seamless crossfade loop point, normalised.
Wind Cliffside Loop
Source: own recording/download. Edited: trimmed to an 18s steady-wind band (avoiding gusts/lulls at the edges), crossfade loop, normalised.
Distant Bell Buoy
Source: "S29-19 Bell buoy.wav" by craigsmith — Freesound ↗ (CC0). Edited: trimmed from the original 90s vintage recording down to a stretch with clear, evenly-spaced dings, crossfade loop.
Ambient Drone (Lamp Room)
Source: "Dark Ambience - Lonely Caverns" by Dredile — Freesound ↗ (CC0, credit given as a courtesy). Edited: trimmed to a stable stretch of the drone, crossfade loop. This was the 11th, optional asset — kept in since there was time to finish it properly.

Foley one-shots

Single non-looping sounds, trimmed tight around the moment itself.

Lamp Mechanism Turn
Source: "Rachet_Joint" by SoundsofTheMachine — Freesound ↗ (CC0). Edited: the raw file contains the same ratchet mechanism recorded at several speeds — picked one rapid action from within the 28s file and trimmed to just that instance.
Footsteps Gravel
Source: "Footsteps Dirt Gravel" by PotatokingXII — Freesound ↗ (CC0). Edited: picked a natural 4-step sequence from the middle of the 35s continuous recording, avoiding the mic-handling noise at the very start.
Rope Creak (Rowboat)
Source: "Mooring Rope.wav" by Rmutt — Freesound ↗ (CC0) — a real mooring rope recording, a near-exact thematic match for the Rowboat's own mooring rope. Edited: scrubbed through the 69s source for the most pronounced groan, trimmed to that window.

Dropped from the original 11

Seagull Call 01, Seagull Call 02, and Pier Wood Creak were sourced (all CC0, logged on the Asset Management page) but not carried through to a finished edit. Their raw sources were already isolated single sounds at 0.4s, 0.7s, and 2.2s respectively — there wasn't a meaningful editing process left to perform or document on files that short in the software being used. 8 finished, fully-documented assets was judged the stronger submission over 11 including 3 where "editing" would have meant nothing more than re-saving the file unchanged.
