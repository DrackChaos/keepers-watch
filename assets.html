3D Assets — Progressive Folio 2

Photo: paws and prints / Unsplash

Two assets are underway for this checkpoint: the Lighthouse Lamp Housing (hero asset, fully modelled and textured) and the Lobster Trap (fully modelled, texture pass still to come). Both galleries below are ordered as a build breakdown — since I only started capturing process shots after most of the geometry was already in place, these are grouped by construction stage rather than a literal undo-history timeline. Each image is captioned with what that stage shows.

Lighthouse Lamp Housing

Status: Fully modelled and textured — the hero asset for this scene.

Lobster Trap

Status: Fully modelled, texture pass not started yet. This is a scope increase from the Folio 1 plan — I'd originally planned to bake the rope weave and wood weathering into the texture rather than modelling it, but ended up building the wire-mesh cage and woven funnel netting as real geometry instead.
