Keeper's Watch

A VR exploration of an abandoned coastal lighthouse — no combat, no objective, just quiet spatial discovery and a story carried by sound.

SIT253 · Progressive Folio 1 · Experience & Exploration theme · Meta Quest 3S

Photo: Evgeni Tcherkasski / Unsplash

The Idea

Keeper's Watch places the user inside an abandoned lighthouse and its immediate grounds at dusk — the lamp room, the cliff path, and the rocky shoreline below. There's no combat and no objective. The whole experience is built around quiet spatial discovery, environmental storytelling (what happened to the keeper?), and an atmosphere carried almost entirely by sound: a distant foghorn, waves against rock, wind across the cliff top.

This site documents the planning stage of the project — Progressive Folio 1 for SIT253. It covers the folio inquiry, research references, visual and sonic moodboards, the planned 3D and audio asset list, asset management and timeline, and a critical reflection on the process so far.
