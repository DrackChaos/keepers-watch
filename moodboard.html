Visual Moodboard

Art style: Stylised low-poly with hand-painted-look textures (flat colour + baked shading, no PBR realism). This is a deliberate choice, not just a beginner shortcut — it fits the Quest 3S triangle budget (see Asset Plan) and matches the painterly, slightly unreal tone of Dear Esther's island.
