Critical Self-Evaluation

Photo: Evgeni Tcherkasski / Unsplash

1. Shift in Perspective

Three weeks into this unit, the biggest change is that I now think about performance limits from the start instead of treating them as a problem for later. Before this, my instinct with any creative project was to build first and worry about constraints after. Planning Keeper's Watch against the Quest 3S's actual triangle and draw-call budget (Meta, n.d.) forced me to design each prop's level of detail before I've modelled anything, which is a different way of working for me.

2. Scoping & Prioritisation

I already simplified the Lobster Trap and Pier Post from my original plan. I'd initially pictured the trap with individually modelled woven rope and the pier posts each showing unique weathering and damage. Realistically, as someone who hasn't done much box modelling yet, that level of per-object detail wasn't a good use of time this early — it's a background/detail prop, not the hero asset. I simplified both to rely more on baked texture detail (rope weave, wood grain, weathering) rather than modelled geometry, which keeps them under budget and achievable without slowing down the Lighthouse Lamp Housing, which is where the real modelling effort should go.

3. Sourcing My Technical Guides

I don't have tutorials bookmarked yet, so as part of this planning stage I looked for reputable ones rather than picking randomly:

  • Blender Guru (Andrew Price) — youtube.com/@blenderguru — widely recommended beginner box-modelling series (his "donut" tutorial is the standard starting point most Blender learners are pointed to).
  • Audacity's own editing documentation — support.audacityteam.org/basics/audacity-editing — for the core editing workflow (import, trim, layer) before touching looping or noise cleanup on the raw sound files.

4. Identifying Skill Gaps

3D modelling basics is what I'm least confident about — I haven't really used Blender before this unit. My plan is to work through the Blender Guru beginner series on a throwaway practice object (not one of my actual folio props) before Week 5, so I've made my early mistakes somewhere that doesn't cost me time on the Lighthouse Lamp Housing itself. If box modelling turns out to take longer than expected, that's exactly why the Lobster Trap and Pier Post are already scoped down — that's my Plan B built in from the start.
